**Week 10 – Real-Estate Analytics Dashboard**:

**About the Project**:
This project builds on the housing data prepared in Week 9. Using Power BI, I created a dashboard to explore house prices, property locations, living areas, and the factors that may affect property values.

**Tools Used**:
Power BI
DAX
Power Query

**Work Done**:

**DAX Measures**:
Created the following measures:
Total Properties Sold – counts distinct property IDs.
Average House Price – calculates the average sale price.
Median Price – calculates the median sale price.
Average Living Area – calculates the average living area in square feet.
Average Price/SqFt – calculates the average price per square foot.

**Dashboard Visuals**:
Geographic Map: Displays property locations using latitude and longitude, with bubble size and color used to represent price information.
Average Price by Bedrooms: Compares average house prices across bedroom counts.
Average Price by Bathrooms: Compares average house prices across bathroom counts.
Average Price by Waterfront Status: Examines the difference in average prices between properties with and without waterfront access.
Average Price by Grade: Compares average prices across property grade categories.
Average Price by Condition: Compares average prices across property condition ratings.
Living Area Slicer: Filters the dashboard by a selected range of living area in square feet.
KPI Cards: Summarize total properties, average house price, median price, average living area, and average price per square foot.

**Outcome**:
The final dashboard brings together key property statistics, geographic information, and price comparisons in one report. The interactive living-area slicer makes it easier to explore properties within a selected size range.

**Previous Work**:
This dashboard uses the cleaned and transformed housing dataset prepared in Week 9 – Housing Data Cleaning & Transformation.
